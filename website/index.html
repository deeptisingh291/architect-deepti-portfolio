BIOGRAPHY & DESIGN ETHOS

Designing with Discipline, Restraint & Material Sincerity

With over 12 years of intensive architectural practice (active in professional design and execution since 2013), Ar. Deepti Singh has established an enduring reputation for thoughtful, climate-responsive structures and refined interiors across Central India. Graduating with First Class honors from CSVTU Bhilai, her work seamlessly integrates pure modernist lines with the functional realities of tropical living.

Her design philosophy rejects superficial decoration in favor of spatial purity: "The client preferred a clean and minimal aesthetic, avoiding excessive colours and textures, with an all-white palette, sunken courtyards, and dedicated green sanctuaries."
